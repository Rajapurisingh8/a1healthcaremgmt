What we do

We build clinical software. Our focus is decision support that runs entirely on the device, so that the quality of reasoning available at a bedside does not depend on the bandwidth in the building.

A1 Healthcare Management Inc is a corporation registered in the United States. Our products are developed against published clinical criteria, implemented as tested code rather than generated output, and distributed through mobile application stores.

Published criteria

Scores and thresholds implemented from the source literature, with unit tests encoding the published boundary conditions.

On-device computation

Clinical calculation runs locally. No clinical data is transmitted, and the software functions with no connectivity.

Stated limits

Where our software shows a model rather than a measurement, it says so on screen. We do not present generated figures as findings.

CliniTwin

A clinical decision-support application for trained healthcare professionals. From bedside observations — vital signs, symptoms, level of consciousness — CliniTwin computes three published early warning scores and returns a structured clinical picture in seconds, entirely on the device.

  • MEWS — Modified Early Warning Score (Subbe et al., QJM 2001)
  • qSOFA — quick Sequential Organ Failure Assessment (Sepsis-3, JAMA 2016)
  • NEWS2 — National Early Warning Score 2 (Royal College of Physicians, 2017), including the single-parameter escalation rule
  • Ranked differential diagnoses with supporting and opposing findings
  • Red flags, immediate actions, and a medication order set with contraindication warnings
CliniTwin is a prototype. It is not a medical device, has not been clinically validated, and must not be used to diagnose, treat, or make care decisions for real patients. All demonstration cases use simulated data.
